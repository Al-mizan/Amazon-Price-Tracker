# 🚀 Amazon Price Tracker & Alert System

Track price drops on Amazon products and get instant email alerts when prices fall below your desired threshold! Perfect for deal hunters 🏷️ and savvy shoppers 🛒.

## 💡 Why You Need This
- **🛒 Stop Wasting Time Checking Prices** – Let the bot do the work 24/7.

- **📉 Buy at the Lowest Price** – Get notified the second a discount drops.

- **📧 Real-Time Email Alerts** – Gmail notifications straight to your inbox.

- **🔒 Secure & Private** – Your credentials stay safe with .env protection.

## 🔥 Features
- **Real-time Price Tracking**: Fetches the latest price from Amazon.
- **Email Notifications**: Sends alerts via Gmail when the price drops.
- **Customizable Threshold**: Set your desired price limit.
- **Secure**: Uses `.env` to protect your email credentials.
